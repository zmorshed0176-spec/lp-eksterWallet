Your Questions, Answered

What Does RFID Stand For, And What Does It Do?
RFID stands for Radio Frequency Identification. It's the contactless technology in your bank cards — and the same technology a thief can use to read them from a distance. Every Ekster has RFID blocking built in.
How does the cardholder work to keep my cards inside it?
Your cards sit inside an aluminium housing held by a patented mechanism. Press the button at the bottom and they fan out one by one; push them back and they lock in place.
What is RFID skimming? How do I protect my cards from it?
Skimming is when someone reads your contactless card wirelessly as they walk past you. The aluminium housing acts as a shield, so your cards can't be read while they're in the wallet.

Yes — every cardholder comes with a 2 year warranty, on top of the 100 day risk-free trial.
